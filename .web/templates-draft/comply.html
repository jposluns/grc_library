Not listed

Answering to something not listed here?

The corpus mappings live in the matrix documents, {{COMPLY_MATRIX_COUNT}} in the corpus, on one page. For how coverage of regulatory obligations is evidenced, read the outcome map {{COMPLY_OUTCOME_LINK}}. Every standard, law, framework, and programme the corpus cites, mapped here or not, is listed with its current edition on the Standards & frameworks page, generated from the canonical citations register. If none of these covers your obligation, the corpus likely does not map it yet.

Known coverage gaps are declared in the {{COMPLY_GAPS_LINK}}, never hidden. Where the corpus does not map your obligation, it says so, and what an assurer ultimately tests is whether your controls are well designed and actually operate, not only whether the paper exists.

Method

Work backwards from the obligation

If your framework has a column in the {{COMPLY_ART_COMPLIANCE_MATRIX}} or the {{COMPLY_ART_FORWARD_MATRIX}}, read off which corpus documents align to its requirements. To work backwards from a control you already hold, the {{COMPLY_ART_REVERSE_MATRIX}} names which library documents address it. Check editions against the {{COMPLY_ART_CITATIONS_REGISTER}} so your programme names the edition your auditor will. Then adopt the supporting documents plus the annex overlays, {{COMPLY_ANNEX_COUNT}} in the corpus, including jurisdiction and sector overlays, and keep the registers that carry the evidence, {{COMPLY_REGISTER_COUNT}} in the corpus.
