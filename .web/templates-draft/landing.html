What it gives you

Starting a governance, risk, or compliance programme means writing dozens of documents before the first control is reviewed. GRC Library gives you {{DOC_TOTAL}} documents that already fit together, each with its owner, approver, and classification, and mapped to the frameworks an auditor will ask about. They are free to adopt and adapt under CC BY-SA 4.0. They are organization-neutral templates: you adapt them with your own names and details and approve them; adopting them confers no certification, and legal advice is outside the library.
