Adapt

How to adapt it

Copy the document and replace the organization-neutral values with your own: thresholds, review cadences, and identifiers. Keep the Owner and Approving Authority as roles, and map each role to a real person in a separate private overlay, rather than editing names into the document itself. Read the related standards the policy points to, where it names any; the policy states intent, and the standards carry the requirements an audit will test. Keep the cross-references between them intact, and record your own version and owner on the adapted copy.
