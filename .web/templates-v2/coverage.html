§03

What we declare we don’t cover yet.

Coverage claims are only as honest as their gaps. Where a leadership concern has no published executive pages yet, it is shown as a declared gap on the decisions index: today that is programme establishment & maturity. And alignment to a framework on this page never means every requirement of that framework is addressed: use the compliance alignment matrix↗ to see, requirement by requirement, what is supported, and what your own gap analysis must still cover. How we decide what to say and check is on the trust page.
