Does this cover the framework or law I answer to?

Name your obligation, follow the mappings to the documents that support it, and assemble the evidence an audit will ask for. The mappings support gap analysis and audit preparation; they do not certify compliance.

Choose your framework or law

The corpus maps its controls to recognized standards, frameworks, and laws in a set of alignment matrices, and cites many more standards across its documents. The rows below name where each starts.

  • ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002Information-security management and controls. Start from the compliance area and the security area.
  • NIST CSF and SP 800-53NIST CSF 2.0 is mapped in a dedicated column in the compliance alignment matrix. SP 800-53 Rev. 5 is not a column in either alignment matrix, but the reverse framework-control crosswalk maps its control families to library documents; the canonical citations register carries the edition each document names.
  • GDPR and privacy lawData protection across jurisdictions, with per-jurisdiction overlays. Start from the privacy area and the annex documents.
  • EU AI Act and AI governance standardsAI law: start from the AI area and its jurisdiction annexes. The Act is mapped in the reverse framework-control crosswalk and appears in the legal-and-regulatory column of the cross-framework alignment matrix, alongside the AI frameworks behind it (ISO/IEC 23894, the NIST AI RMF).
  • Cloud and supply-chain frameworksCloud control matrices and third-party assurance. Start from the supply-chain area and the matrix documents.
  • Risk and AI frameworksISO 31000, ISO/IEC 23894, NIST SP 800-39, the NIST AI RMF, COBIT 2019, and CSA CCM v4.1 are mapped in the cross-framework alignment matrix. Start from the risk area.

Answering to something not listed here? The mappings live in a small set of matrices, chiefly the compliance alignment matrix (the CSA control matrices, ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF, and the trade programmes) and the cross-framework alignment matrix (ISO 31000, ISO/IEC 23894, NIST SP 800-39, the NIST AI RMF, COBIT 2019, and CSA CCM v4.1). To work backwards from a control you already hold, the reverse framework-control crosswalk names which library documents address it. Laws such as the GDPR and the EU AI Act are mapped in that reverse crosswalk and carried in the legal-and-regulatory column of the cross-framework matrix, as well as covered in the jurisdiction annexes. If your obligation is not covered by any of these, the corpus likely does not map it yet.

Work backwards from the obligation

If your framework appears in the compliance alignment matrix or the cross-framework alignment matrix, read off which corpus documents align to its requirements. A law such as the GDPR or the EU AI Act is mapped in the reverse framework-control crosswalk and the legal-and-regulatory column of the cross-framework matrix, and covered in its jurisdiction annex. To work backwards from a control you already hold, the reverse framework-control crosswalk names which documents address it. Check editions against the canonical citations register so your programme names the library's current canonical edition. Then adopt the supporting documents plus the annex overlays for your jurisdiction or sector, and keep the registers that carry the evidence.
