Meet an obligation

Does this cover the framework or law I answer to?

Name your obligation, follow the mappings to the documents that support it, and assemble the evidence an audit will ask for. The mappings support gap analysis and audit preparation; they do not certify compliance.

Choose your framework or law

The corpus maps its controls to recognized standards, frameworks, and laws, including the ones below. Every mapping resolves through the compliance area and the alignment matrix.

  • ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002Information-security management and controls. Start from the compliance area and the security area.
  • NIST CSF and SP 800-53The cybersecurity framework and its control catalogue. Both resolve through the compliance alignment matrix.
  • GDPR and privacy lawData protection across jurisdictions, with per-jurisdiction overlays. Start from the privacy area and the annex documents.
  • EU AI Act and AI governance standardsAI law and the ISO/IEC and NIST AI frameworks behind it. Start from the AI area and its jurisdiction annexes.
  • Cloud and supply-chain frameworksCloud control matrices and third-party assurance. Start from the supply-chain area and the matrix documents.
  • Risk management standardsRisk methodology, assessment, and treatment. Start from the risk area.

Answering to something not listed here? The alignment matrix is the complete, current mapping; if your obligation is absent there, the corpus does not map it yet.

Work backwards from the obligation

Find your framework or law in the compliance alignment matrix and read off which corpus documents align to its requirements. Check editions against the canonical citations register, so your programme names the edition your auditor will. Then adopt the supporting documents plus the annex overlays for your jurisdiction or sector, and keep the registers that carry the evidence.
