How to adapt it

Copy the document and replace the organization-neutral values with your own: thresholds, review cadences, and identifiers. Keep the Owner and Approving Authority as roles, and map each role to a real person in a private overlay you do not publish, rather than editing names into a public copy. Read the related standards the policy points to, where present; the policy states intent, and the standards carry the requirements an audit will test. Keep the cross-references between them intact, and record your own version and owner on the adapted copy.
