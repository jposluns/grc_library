How to adapt it

Copy the document and replace the organization-neutral values with your own: names, roles, thresholds, and review cadences. Read the governing standard the policy delegates to; the policy states intent, and the standard carries the requirements an audit will test. Keep the cross-references between them intact, and record your own version and owner on the adapted copy.
