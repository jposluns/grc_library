What do I need for the problem in front of me?

Start from the problem or the lifecycle event, not the paperwork. Each one below routes to the areas and document types that resolve it.

Common problems and events

Secure privileged access

Admin accounts, service accounts, and the people who can change everything. The controls live in the security area; the monitoring that proves they operate lives in operations.

See it end to end: the privileged-access journey walks one control from policy intent to independent assurance.

Respond to an incident

Detection, coordination, communication, and recovery. The resilience area carries the response and recovery plans; the operations area carries the monitoring that detects the incident in the first place.

Rehearse the bad day: the identity-provider compromise scenario walks leadership through one.

Govern an AI system end to end

Intake, approval, oversight, incidents, and retirement, governed like any other system. The AI area is a self-contained sub-corpus: frameworks, standards, procedures, registers, templates, and per-jurisdiction annexes.

See it end to end: the AI idea-to-retirement journey follows the governed life of one system.

Manage a supplier

Onboarding, ongoing monitoring, concentration, and exit. The supply-chain area carries the controls; the registers carry the inventory and the assurance trail.

Rehearse the failure: the critical-supplier outage scenario shows what the day itself looks like.

Retain and destroy records

Classification, retention, cross-border movement, and provable destruction. The security area carries classification and handling; the privacy area carries the obligations that set the clock.

See it end to end: the record retention-and-destruction journey follows one record from rule to evidence.

The journeys and scenarios linked above read at source on GitHub today; on-site versions are coming.
