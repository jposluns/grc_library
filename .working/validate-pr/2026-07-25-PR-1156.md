> - **Publication date**: ISO 8601 year or year-month where known.

`2025-06` conforms. **The previous `2025-06-22` did not**, because the convention admits a year or a

that the cell was non-conforming in FORMAT regardless of who the authority is**, and normalizing to
year-month both conforms and drops exactly the precision no held text supports.

That is a strictly better fix than my verdict, and it did not come from the orchestrator alone: the
independent verifier's Check 4 upheld my authority reasoning ("the Texas TRAIGA no-change verdict is
SOUND on authority") and then added the note the orchestrator acted on, "the date cell's precision
exceeds the convention's stated format". So the chain worked exactly as intended, with the
independent reader catching what the drafter missed. **PASS**, and I record my verdict as superseded.
