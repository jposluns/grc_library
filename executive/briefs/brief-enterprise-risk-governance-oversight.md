## Why this matters

Enterprise risk is governed only if the governing body can see it operating, not merely be assured it does. The question is not whether the organization has a risk function, but whether risk is identified, scored on a consistent scale, judged against an appetite this body set, treated or formally accepted with an owner, and reported to the board on a cadence, with each step visible in a record rather than a reassurance. Each of those expectations maps to a specific artefact in the risk corpus that an executive can ask to see.

## What the corpus establishes

- The [enterprise governance and risk-management policy](../../risk/policy-enterprise-governance-and-risk-management.md) is the **dependency** of the programme: it sets the framework, principles, roles, and accountability that the other controls require; the governing mandate precedes any control to govern.
- The [enterprise risk-management standard](../../risk/standard-enterprise-risk-management.md) makes a **contribution** to consistent evaluation: its risk taxonomy and scoring methodology give units a common scale on which risks become comparable.
- The [key-risk-indicator register](../../risk/register-key-risk-indicators.md) makes a **contribution** to early warning: it defines the indicators, thresholds, and escalation that surface a risk moving before it breaches its tolerance.
- The [risk appetite statement](../../risk/template-risk-appetite-statement.md) is a **dependency** of any within-appetite or out-of-appetite judgement: that judgement requires the appetite and its tolerance boundaries to exist first.
- The [enterprise risk register](../../risk/template-enterprise-risk-register.md) defines the **evidence** record for the risk state: the schema in which each risk's score, treatment, and residual position are captured.
- The [risk-acceptance procedure](../../risk/procedure-risk-acceptance.md) is a **prevention** control against silent acceptance: a mandatory stop at which an undocumented residual risk is documented, owned, and time-bound rather than absorbed.
- The [board risk report](../../risk/template-board-risk-report.md) makes a **contribution** to informed oversight: it structures the periodic flow of risk information to the board for challenge.
- The [assurance map](../../risk/register-assurance-map.md) makes a **contribution** to seeing where assurance over material risk has gaps, on a defined rating scale.
- The [exception and risk-acceptance management policy](../../governance/policy-exception-and-risk-acceptance-management.md) is the **dependency** for a governed deviation: it requires each exception to be risk-assessed, time-bound, approved, and centrally tracked rather than unrecorded.

## What this means for the organization

- **Require an appetite this body set, and require risk reported against it.** An appetite no one owns, or a register never read against it, stops before the corpus starts.
- **Expect evidence, not assurances.** The register extract, the acceptance records with expiries or review triggers, the completed board report, and the assurance-map ratings are evidence classes; a control the organization cannot evidence is not demonstrably operating.
- **A formally accepted residual risk is a governed decision, not a silent absorption.** Acceptances carry owners, compensating controls, and an expiry or event-based review trigger.
- **Risks scored on one consistent scale can be aggregated to a board view.** A register scored ad hoc cannot.

## Evidence to request

- The current enterprise risk-register extract, with scores, treatment, and residual position.
- The approved risk appetite statement, and the register read against it.
- The risk-acceptance records, each with an owner, compensating controls, and an expiry or review trigger.
- The latest board risk report, and the actions and follow-ups it captured.
- The current assurance-map ratings for the material risks, and any open items in the enterprise exception register.

## Limitations

This page is a non-normative executive narrative that creates no compliance by itself; the linked corpus documents govern, and where this page and a corpus document differ, the corpus document prevails. It routes to the corpus rather than reproducing its values: the appetite categories and tolerance boundaries, the scoring-matrix bands and rating thresholds, the register field schema, the acceptance durations and approval-authority levels, the assurance rating scale, and the board-report cadence live in the linked documents and are not restated here. This page carries composite claims: the division of labour it describes across the risk policy, standard, register, indicators, appetite, acceptance, reporting, and assurance controls requires validation by the adopting organization against its own arrangements.
