## Context for these questions

Each question below is paired with the operating evidence a strong answer produces, and routes to the corpus document that governs it. The method is deliberate: a governing body learns more from the evidence a control actually generates than from an assurance that the control exists. A described control the organization cannot evidence is not demonstrably operating. The questions are grouped by the governance exposures the crypto corpus is built around; a strong answer to any of them reads from a live record, not a summary of one.

## Questions

**Theme: classification and approval. Do we govern crypto activity before it starts?**

- *For every crypto-asset activity we run, was the asset classified and the activity approved before it began?* A strong answer produces the [crypto-asset inventory](../../crypto/register-crypto-asset-inventory.md) entries showing the classification result and the activity-approval record dated before the activity started, which the [crypto-asset governance framework](../../crypto/framework-crypto-asset-governance.md) makes the precondition in its governance requirements (no asset issued, offered, custodied, traded, or used before it is classified and approved).
- *How would we find crypto activity that never came through the approval gate?* A strong answer describes a control operating, not a policy forbidding it: the reconciliation of the inventory against on-chain balances and platform accounts that the [inventory register](../../crypto/register-crypto-asset-inventory.md) maintains as its authoritative record.

**Theme: custody. Are client and proprietary assets safeguarded and segregated?**

- *Can we show client crypto-assets are segregated from our own and beyond our creditors' reach?* A strong answer produces the custody policy, the on-ledger separate positions, and the legal-segregation basis the [digital-asset custody standard](../../crypto/standard-digital-asset-custody.md) requires (its segregation and register-of-positions sections).
- *Can no single person move client assets, and are custody keys under the key-lifecycle controls?* A strong answer shows the split-knowledge and dual-control evidence and the key-lifecycle records the [custody standard](../../crypto/standard-digital-asset-custody.md) sets for wallet tiering and key custody.

**Theme: reserves and redemption (stable-value tokens). Is the backing real and matched to claims?**

- *If we issue a stable-value token, is the reserve constituted, segregated, independently audited, and matched to holder claims?* A strong answer produces the reserve policy, the segregation evidence, and the independent reserve-audit result the [reserve and prudential standard](../../crypto/standard-crypto-asset-reserve-and-prudential-requirements.md) requires (its reserve-constitution and segregation sections).
- *Do we hold recovery and redemption plans for the token's stressed and end-of-life states?* A strong answer produces the notified recovery plan and redemption plan the [reserve standard](../../crypto/standard-crypto-asset-reserve-and-prudential-requirements.md) requires (its recovery-and-redemption-plan section).

**Theme: disclosure and filings. Are holders informed and authorizations current?**

- *For each public offer or admission, is there a compliant, current white paper on the right lifecycle path for the asset class?* A strong answer produces the white paper and its notification, approval, or publication record on the class-appropriate path the [white-paper content and disclosure standard](../../crypto/standard-crypto-asset-white-paper-disclosure.md) sets.
- *Are our authorizations current, and are the ongoing regulatory obligations maintained?* A strong answer produces the submission file and the log of maintained records, policies, and notifications the [regulatory-submission standard](../../crypto/standard-crypto-asset-regulatory-submission.md) sets.

**Theme: providers and smart contracts. Are dependencies vetted and controlled?**

- *For each crypto-asset service provider we rely on, did the authorization gate pass; and for each deployed or relied-on smart contract, is the upgrade or change authority named and controlled?* A strong answer produces the provider authorization and vetting record the [service-provider vetting standard](../../crypto/standard-crypto-asset-service-provider-vetting.md) requires at its legal-identity-and-authorization gate, and the deployment and change-governance record the [smart-contract and oracle risk standard](../../crypto/standard-smart-contract-and-oracle-risk.md) sets for immutability-aware deployment and upgrade governance.

**A weak answer in any theme:** an assurance in place of a record. "We hold mostly the same assets as last year" with no classification date; "the custodian handles segregation" with no evidence the organization verified it; "the reserve is fine" with no independent audit; a white paper that was published once and never updated as the offer changed.

## Evidence to request

- The crypto-asset inventory with per-asset classification and pre-activity approval, reconciled against on-chain balances.
- The custody policy, the register of positions, and the key-custody split-knowledge and dual-control evidence.
- For each stable-value token, the reserve composition, the independent reserve-audit result, and the recovery and redemption plans.
- The white-paper record and its notification, approval, or publication evidence for each offer or admission.
- The provider authorization and vetting records, and the smart-contract deployment and change-governance records.
