## Scenario premise

A monitored material risk drifts. An early-warning indicator crosses its threshold, and on re-scoring the risk's residual position moves above the tolerance the governing body set. It walks the event through the controls the risk corpus sets, to show a governing body where each engages and what evidence a well-run response leaves behind.

## How the event unfolds

The indicator was watched precisely because the risk it tracks matters. Its threshold is crossed, which is a signal, not yet a decision: the breach is either detected, escalated, and resolved through a governed decision, or noticed late and absorbed without one. This scenario follows the governed path and shows where each corpus control engages.

## Where the corpus controls engage

- **Detection.** The [key-risk-indicator register](../../risk/register-key-risk-indicators.md) is where the breach is flagged and escalated, through the thresholds and escalation it defines (its reporting-and-escalation section).
- **The tolerance breached.** The breach exists relative to the appetite the governing body set; the [risk appetite statement](../../risk/template-risk-appetite-statement.md) is the instrument that defines those tolerance boundaries.
- **Re-scoring.** The [enterprise risk-management standard](../../risk/standard-enterprise-risk-management.md) scoring methodology re-rates the risk and shows it has crossed the band.
- **Recording and escalation.** The [risk-register procedure](../../risk/procedure-risk-register.md) is where the re-score, the escalation, and the entry's status are recorded and carried through to closure.
- **The governed decision.** Where the decision is to accept the residual, the [risk-acceptance procedure](../../risk/procedure-risk-acceptance.md) requires an owner, compensating controls, and an expiry or event-based review trigger, and, where a control exception is involved, the [exception and risk-acceptance management policy](../../governance/policy-exception-and-risk-acceptance-management.md) records it at the authority it sets; the decision is a governed one rather than a silent absorption.
- **Board visibility.** The breach, the decision, and any board decision requested surface in the organization's board reporting, structured per the [board risk report](../../risk/template-board-risk-report.md), on its cadence.
- **Assurance gaps.** Where the breach reveals thin assurance over the risk, the [assurance map](../../risk/register-assurance-map.md) is where that gap is visible.

## What good looks like

The indicator flagged the breach rather than a later event revealing it. The re-score and the escalation were recorded through the register procedure, not held informally. The choice was a governed one, a treatment plan or a formal acceptance with an owner, compensating controls, and an expiry or event-based review trigger, rather than a silent absorption. The breach and the decision reached the governing body on its cadence. Each of those is a control the organization can point to and evidence.

## Evidence to request

- The key-risk-indicator breach record and the Red KRI Escalation Report.
- The register entry showing the re-score and the crossed tolerance boundary.
- The treatment plan, or the acceptance records (owner, compensating controls, and an expiry or review trigger) and any related time-bound exception records (owner and an expiry, where a control exception is involved).
- The board risk report carrying the breach and the decision, and the actions and follow-ups it captured.
- The assurance-map rating for the affected risk.
