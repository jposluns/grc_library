## Purpose

This annex defines privacy and AI regulatory requirements applicable to the processing of personal data in Indonesia under the Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) No. 27 of 2022. It supplements the Privacy and Data Governance Policy and the Privacy Impact and Cross-Border Transfer Procedure.

---

## Applicable laws and regulatory authorities

- **Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) No. 27 of 2022**: Enacted 17 October 2022, with a two-year period for controllers and processors to conform (Article 74), which ended in October 2024. Indonesia's first dedicated and comprehensive personal data protection law. An implementing Government Regulation (reported as No. 33 of 2026, enacted July 2026 and effective from January 2027) is not held in the reference base, so an adopter confirms its text and effective dates directly.
- Data Controller and Processor framework aligned with GDPR concepts.
- Rights: access, correction, portability, restriction, erasure, withdrawal of consent, and objection.
- Mandatory breach notification: on a failure of personal data protection, the controller notifies the data subject and the data protection institution in writing within 3 x 24 hours (UU PDP Article 46(1)).
- Consent required from parent or guardian for children's personal data.
- **Regulatory authority:** the UU PDP provides for a data protection institution; as of 2026 it remains in formation, with functions exercised in practice by the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs. The UU PDP (held in the reference base in its Indonesian original) provides that the institution is established by and answers to the President (Article 58); an adopter confirms the current institutional arrangement directly.

---

## AI and privacy obligations

- A data subject may object to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that has legal effects or significantly affects the data subject (UU PDP Article 10(1)); the detailed procedure is left to a Government Regulation (Article 10(2)).
- Indonesia has no binding AI-specific regulation held in the reference base as of 2026; an adopter confirms the current state of national AI-governance instruments directly.
- Purpose limitation and data minimization principles apply to AI systems processing personal data.

---

## Cross-border transfer mechanisms

- Transfers subject to equivalent protection standard in the recipient jurisdiction.
- An approved country list is under development.
- Contractual safeguards serve as the primary interim mechanism.

---

## Enforcement and fines

- Administrative fines: up to 2% of annual Indonesian-derived revenue.
- Criminal penalties: up to IDR 60 billion for serious violations, including imprisonment.
